**PROPOSAL against a VENDORED (MIRROR) standard — placed here rather than made, because `docs/standards/research/` must not be edited in this repo and an amendment goes upstream to MDC-Master-Planning then re-vendors. Same mechanism as C-d5m6c9pu.** **The consequence, and it is why this is a finding rather than a note:** §3's verbatim rule (`research_standard.md:106`) says *"A span may be labelled or presented as a quotation only if its exact character sequence was returned by a fetch"* and stops there. It never says how a paper DECLARES the transformations that unavoidably sit between a source's bytes and the paper's rendering of them — and they are unavoidable: markdown and asciidoc inline markup inside a quoted span (`[share-nothing](http://…)`, `<<def_SHA1,SHA-1>>`, `_30 days_`), source line-wrap and sentence-boundary double spaces, typographic apostrophes and quotes in `pdftotext` output, and whether an elision is marked at the end of a span as well as inside it. **Undeclared, a verifier re-derives the whole transformation set from scratch and reports a FALSE MISS on every span whose deviation it has not yet guessed.** **Measured on this PR, and the shape of the measurement is the argument:** `cross_node_memory_protocol.md` took **five** independent critic passes, and **after round 1 not one blocking evidence defect existed** — no fabricated source, no fabricated span, no miscitation, no inflated mark, no wrong count, across 28 sources and 90 quoted spans, each git-hosted span verified by clone-and-`grep -F`. **Rounds 2, 3 and 4 each found a defect in the CONVENTION, never in the evidence.** The paper's original blanket claim (*"the exact characters returned — the two exceptions are marked"*) was over-absolute; its four-rule replacement was incomplete by two whole classes (PDF typographic marks, and local-file spans); that replacement's rule 4 was then over-absolute in exactly the way the blanket claim had been, with 19 counter-examples; fixing rule 4 left rule 2 asserting a universal its own new text falsified. **The cost is not hypothetical:** round 2's sweep reported sound spans as deviations, and the analyst's own truncation detector returned 39, then 23, before reaching the true 19. **So nearly the entire correction budget on a paper whose evidence was clean went to inventing a convention no standard asked for — and the next paper reinvents it or goes without.** **Done-state today: yes, and small** — a clause in §3 beside the verbatim rule requiring a paper to state the normalisation between its sources' bytes and its own rendering, each rule scoped to what was measured, its exceptions named, and its counts' populations stated. **This PR's §6 is a worked exemplar to draw from, including its failure mode, which is the half worth codifying: a normalisation stated as a UNIVERSAL generates false findings and is worse than none.** **NOT a defect:** nothing behaves wrongly — §3 is silent, not incorrect, the paper conforms today, and its `Critic:` verdict is `PASS`. **Not an expansion of C-d5m6c9pu:** that concerns §2's sizing rubric and cycle SHAPE; this concerns §3's sourcing rules. Different section, disjoint remedy, neither subsumes the other. **Not an expansion of C-zwzepum0:** that class is a claim that is FALSE about another file. Every span here was TRUE — what was missing was any stated way to CHECK it. **Not filed as an issue** — capability that would be ADDED to a document, which per [`finding-routing.md` § 4](../../finding-routing.md) makes it a candidate whatever its done-state looks like, and the default is against filing.
