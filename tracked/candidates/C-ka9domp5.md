**The consequence, not the discrepancy.** The bag's `bag-info.txt` tag namespace is PMP's artifact, and [WD Phase 5](../../../development/workflow-decomposition/phase5_configuration_a_run_absorbed.md) r1 is the first outside component to write a field into it. PMP answered that with **r13, four clauses inside its own phase doc** ([`phase3_the_emit_rule.md`](../../../development/persistent-memory-protocol/phase3_the_emit_rule.md), requirement 13): who may add a tag, what a reader does with an unrecognised one, whether bag metadata carries its own version, and by what mechanism an outside component contributes one. **Every one of those four questions is generic** — nothing in them is about bags, tags or BagIt — yet the answers live where only PMP readers will find them. **So the second component to do this reads nothing and decides again**, and two components answering *"what does a reader do with a field it does not recognise"* differently in one repo is a compatibility break nobody reviews, because neither side is wrong in its own document. **Evidence:** r13's four clauses at the phase doc above; the trigger is WD Phase 5 r1; the shared-artifact seam is already written from both sides at [PMP roadmap § *What a SIBLING COMPONENT requires*](../../../development/persistent-memory-protocol/roadmap.md). **⚠ The "one instance" premise is contestable, and it is what makes remedy (b) look cheap — so rule on it first.** [`finding-routing.md`](../../finding-routing.md) § 7 is already a repo-altitude table that, per shared artifact, names the mechanism and the single writer, and its blockquote rules the `decision`/`status` writer split on `direction.md` and this very file. That answers r13's clause (a) *who may add* and clause (d) *by what mechanism an outside party contributes*, for two other shared artifacts, in a binding standard. **If that is prior art, the instance count is two and not one**; if it is not, the reason is substantive and belongs in the ruling. **Two remedies and they are different rulings.** (a) **Promote the four questions to a standard** on cross-component field-namespace writes, leaving r13 as the bag's instantiation of it — costs a new standards doc, and how much it costs depends on the premise above. (b) **Wait for the second instance** and promote then, accepting that the second component will have already chosen — cheaper now, and the thing being deferred is precisely the compatibility the rule exists to protect. **Done-state today: yes** for either — the ruling is which, not when. **`component` is `persistent-memory-protocol`** because the bag is PMP's artifact and r13 is PMP's requirement; the finding is that the rule is stated at component altitude rather than repo altitude, so the evidence sits inside a named component even though the remedy may not.
