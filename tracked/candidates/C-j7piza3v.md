**A THIRD blind spot in the same guard, found by PR #96's `build-refine` correction pass and recorded here rather than as a sibling row because the mechanism is the one this row is already about:** the guard derives the map's PATHS from `git ls-files` and asserts parse, enumeration and reachability, and says nothing about whether a continuation-annotation block is still ATTACHED to the leaf it describes. Four instances inside this PR's lifetime — one repaired at `66be084` (introduced by `d34caab`), and three live and repaired at this row's own pass, of which two were introduced by merges into this branch and one (`test_run_log.py`, introduced by `e7fadd9` for PR #82) has been wrong on `origin/main` since 2026-08-12. Same mechanism as the duplicate above — something inserts a leaf where nothing is looking. **The candidate tell is measured, not asserted, and its METHOD is stated so a triage pass can re-derive it rather than trust it:** over every line matching `[├└]──\s` that carries a `#`, take the annotation text after the `#` and flag the leaf when that text ends in a comma or in a word that cannot end an English sentence (`the a an and of to with from against into per via that whose which these those its their`) AND the NEXT line is another leaf rather than a continuation. Across all 680 lines that predicate returned **3 hits, all 3 true positives, zero false**; widening the list to sentence-capable words (`on it is are`) returned **6 hits for the same 3 true**, so the precision comes from the word list rather than from the structure. **It is NOT fit to ship as a gate as it stands** — validated on n=3 against a hand-curated English vocabulary that must grow as annotation phrasing drifts, with no bound on false negatives. A sound gate needs a STRUCTURAL signal, which the current format does not offer, and that is the real finding. **The `#`-column tell is not gateable at all, and that was measured too:** of the **294** annotated leaves, **34** carry `#` past column 53, and in **29** of those the filename itself already ends at or past column 53 — so the column measures name length, not displacement, and the remaining 5 are ordinary extra padding. A guard keyed on column would be 34 false positives deep before it caught anything. Consequence: `CLAUDE.md` calls the map authoritative, and `_map_entries()` in `test_retired_vocabulary_is_gone_from_live_surfaces.py` joins each leaf to its continuation lines, so a severed block silently re-attributes one guard's guarantees to another in that sweep's per-entry text — checked and NOT a live wrong answer from that gate, because all seven paths involved return `None` from `_is_record`, so no RECORD exemption is misapplied. **SPLIT OUT OF C-npx1uwgj at PR #96 disposition pass 8**, because its verdict is the opposite of that row's other two: they are measured and shippable, this one says in its own words that it *"is NOT fit to ship as a gate as it stands"*. **The remedy is stated and has been homeless until now:** a PATH-KEYED annotation source of truth with the ASCII tree as a RENDERED VIEW, surfaced by `refactoring-evaluator` at pass 8, which makes displacement structurally impossible rather than merely detectable — the STRUCTURAL signal this row says the format does not offer. It was reported with no pointer because pass 7's dispatch barred adding a candidate row; that homelessness was the reviewer's and is corrected here rather than left to die at merge.
