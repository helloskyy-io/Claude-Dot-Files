**The synthesis names both as *"genuine research questions"* and *"the strongest candidates for a full cycle on this topic"*, and neither has a home.** They are placed as ONE row because one research cycle answers both — same corpus, same pool, and the format question is what determines which fields a classification rule would even have to range over; splitting them would commission two sweeps of the same literature. **The consequence, and it is why this is a finding rather than a note:** the classification half is a **hard gate on [Phase 7](../../docs/development/persistent-memory-protocol/roadmap.md#phase-7--cross-machine-aggregation-writing-locally-firstphase7s3aggregationmd)** — shipping raw records to a store every edge reads crosses a trust boundary nobody has drawn, and the [heartbeat pollution paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.23064) measured pollution reaching durable memory at rates up to **91% without prompt injection**, ordinary misinformation sufficing. Unresearched, the gate is opened by whoever happens to be building at the time, from priors. **THE CLASSIFICATION HALF IS TWO QUESTIONS, AND COLLAPSING THEM IS HOW THE SECOND GETS SKIPPED — surfaced by this plan's security review and worth carrying into the cycle's scope.** *(a) EGRESS: what may leave this edge* — the per-field classification above. *(b) INGRESS: what may this edge BELIEVE* — how a reader treats another edge's records, whether they are origin-authenticated, and what a reader may *act on* versus merely display. **The heartbeat paper is evidence for (b), not (a):** pollution is an *integrity* threat, so a build could classify every field correctly and still ship a reader that treats a polluted record as fleet history — and under the plan's rebuild test the stores are **projections**, so that record replays straight into this file. A BagIt manifest does not close it either: it is regenerable by any writer, so it proves integrity against accident and transport corruption, not against a party with write access — which is exactly the party a shared store introduces. **One cycle answers both; the row names both so satisfying one cannot be mistaken for satisfying the other.** **The question is smaller than it first looked and that is itself a research input:** synthesis §11 records that §2 changed its shape — every byte in the journal was *deliberately* written to a durable surface and most is already public in a GitHub PR, so the question is **per-field, and most fields answer themselves**. It is still a decision. **Done-state today: yes** — a research cycle has one, and the pool exists (`docs/development/persistent-memory-protocol/research/`). **Not an expansion of C-2xgap6o1:** that is deployment automation, a delivery domain. This is evidence for a memory-component gate. Disjoint remedies, different consumers. **Not a defect:** nothing behaves wrongly; no record crosses a boundary today. **Not filed as an issue** — commissioning research is capability that would be ADDED, which per [`finding-routing.md` § 4](../../docs/standards/finding-routing.md) makes it a candidate whatever its done-state looks like.
