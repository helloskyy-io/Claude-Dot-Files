**Measured in this tree, not carried on a prior pass's account.** `build_refine` holds 30 substantive prompt blocks and `build_refine_minor` holds 7; **25 of the major tier's blocks have no counterpart in the minor tier** above a 0.55 similarity floor. Most are the documented lens-count axis — the Stage-2a/2b machinery, the refactoring-evaluator and standards-auditor briefs, the quality-control dispatch — and those SHOULD differ. **Ten are not**, and they are the finding: `MUTATE AN ASSERTION'S SCOPE`, `COMPARE THE CHECK SET`, `PRINT WHAT THE MUTATION ACTUALLY PRODUCED`, `SIZE THE CHANGE THEN SET THE BAR`, `ASK WHAT EACH GUARD DOES NOT LOOK AT`, `A MUTATION PROVES A GUARD DISCRIMINATES WITHIN ITS POPULATION`, *"If you have written the remedy, apply it"*, *"Rejecting is legitimate — with reasoning that holds"*, the treat-another-run's-*"pre-existing"*-as-a-claim paragraph, and the finding-disposition rule. None of those is about how many lenses a pass runs. **THE CLASS IS FLEET-WIDE, which no prior pass measured:** the same asymmetry holds in the other two tier pairs — `build_draft` 17 major-only against 27 minor-only, `research_write` 18 against 16 — so the minor tier is not simply a subset of the major one in either direction, and whatever rule is written has three consumers rather than one. **Why NO GUARD CAN REACH IT, which is what separates this from what PR #100 shipped.** Both block detectors in `test_tier_siblings_do_not_DRIFT_by_a_sentence` compare PAIRS, and an orphan block has no pair; the module's own *WHAT THIS DOES NOT LOOK AT* names it *"the LARGEST uncovered class, not a corner"*. The `_orphan_lines` detector that PR added reaches one granularity down — a whole LINE missing from a block that DOES have a counterpart — and is deliberately not this. **Why it is a PROPOSAL and not a defect:** every one of the 25 predates the branch, nothing about the promotion created them, and reconciling any of them requires first deciding what the minor tier is FOR — a contract stated today only in code comments and binding nowhere. **THE TEN DO NOT SHARE ONE BLOCKER, and triage should not be told they do.** Most wait on that contract. But at least two — *"If you have written the remedy, apply it"* and *"Rejecting is legitimate — with reasoning that holds"* — are disposition rules, a category this PR already treated as tier-invariant when it promoted five `resolve_*` fragments; their real obstacle is that each is already frozen with two consumers in `test_prompt_blocks_are_shared_not_copied.py`, so adding a third tier trips the spread check and the correct move is a cross-family promotion rather than a contract ruling. A contract ruling alone would leave those two exactly where they are. **Done-state today: yes**, and it is a paragraph in `workflow-scripts.md` saying which classes of prompt content are tier-invariant (operational safety, disposition rules, evidence discipline) and which are tier-scoped (review depth, agent count), after which the ten are a mechanical reconciliation and a guard becomes writable. **The cost of leaving it:** this is the third correction pass on one PR to meet the same question and the third to answer it differently, and the instance that motivated the hold — the minor refine tier never being told to re-check `origin/main` before pushing — shipped for the whole life of the branch behind a baseline entry that recorded the pair as looked-at. **Not an expansion of C-ll2j5ut7, and they should be ruled together.** C-ll2j5ut7 asks whether a surfacing-only GUARD may exist under that section at all; this asks what the two tiers must CONTAIN. Different questions, same section, and a clause written for either without reading the other is how one section grows two rules that disagree. **Not C-lg7lpge9 either** — that is about a promotion silently editing prose, which is a fidelity question rather than a content one. **Predicate, stated so triage can re-derive rather than trust:** blocks are the blank-line-separated runs of every `prompts/*.md` under a child, excluding the shared pool, keeping those of 120 bytes or more — the same split `test_tier_siblings_do_not_DRIFT_by_a_sentence.py` uses; a block is an orphan when its best `difflib` ratio against every block of the sibling is under 0.55. **Lens verdicts (gate 5).** `/decide`: the five-whys bottoms out at *nobody ever wrote down what the `_minor` tier is for*, so each reconciliation is argued from the arguer's model of it — which is why three passes on one PR reached three different answers. It does NOT dissolve: the ambiguity is real, load-bearing and cheap to settle, and settling it converts a recurring judgement into a lookup. `/best-practices`: a shared-partial system states a contract once and names which partials are invariant — the standard practice is an explicit inventory of what varies by tier, not a convention inferred from what happens to differ today; the drift here is what an unstated contract predicts. *(Id allocated by re-reading this file and `git show origin/main:docs/standards/architecture/research/candidates.md`, whose maximum is C-j7piza3v; C-lg7lpge9 and C-ll2j5ut7 are this PR's own earlier rows.)*
