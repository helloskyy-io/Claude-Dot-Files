**Measured on this PR, by re-running the comparison the PR body claims: the claim was false and nothing in the tree could see it.** PR #100's body asserts *"Every one of the 8 affected prompts was snapshotted before any edit and reassembles byte-identical after promotion."* Rendering all eight at `origin/main` and at the branch head and diffing them gave **6 of 8 differing** — two draft prompts carried 447 bytes of `<!-- SHARED -->` editor commentary into the model-facing text, one of it as literal line 1, and seven promotion seams each gained a blank line. **The draft was not being careless: its check verified that the MOVED TEXT was unchanged, while its claim asserted that the ASSEMBLED PROMPT was unchanged, and nothing bridged the two.** That gap is the proposal. **The consequence is not this PR's headers — those are fixed here — it is the next promotion.** Seven duplicated consumer-sets remain, each a future PR that will reach for the same methodology, and `workflow-scripts.md` § *A prompt block with two consumers is promoted* names the mechanical recipe without naming any fidelity check at all. Every existing guard is blind to the class: the duplication test matches verbatim blocks, the budget test counts file bytes, `test_prompt_completeness` matches supplier names, and `test_promoted_fragments_render_for_every_consumer` (shipped by this PR) asserts a needle ARRIVED, never that nothing else did. **A promotion that silently rewords a sentence, flattens an indented literal, or drags commentary into the prompt passes all of them** — and the draft's own reflection records that it nearly shipped exactly the flattening defect, caught only by the throwaway script. **Done-state today: yes, and the mechanism already exists in the tree** — this pass's comparison drove the real entry points with a captured `run_claude` and normalised the tmpdir, which is the same shape `test_promoted_fragments_render_for_every_consumer.py` already uses; what it needs to become a gate is a stored pre-promotion snapshot and a rule for retiring one. **The design question is real and is triage's to rule, not this row's:** a committed snapshot per promotion PR, versus a helper the promoter runs against `origin/main` at authoring time and pastes the output of, versus retiring *"byte-identical"* as a claim a PR body may make without a reproducible check behind it. The third is free and the weakest. **PROPOSAL, not a defect:** no prompt is wrong today — this PR's two header instances are fixed in it, and the remaining seam blank lines are disclosed and inert. What does not exist is the check. **Raised twice independently, which is why it is filed rather than surfaced:** the draft's own reflection proposed *"a small helper that snapshots the affected prompts, promotes, and diffs the reassembly"* and deliberately did not place it, and `quality-control` reached the same remedy from the opposite direction — that an unreproducible check makes the surrounding rigour read as more trustworthy than it is. **Not an expansion of C-ifs47g3r** (promote the research family's RULES footer — that is one more promotion to DO; this is the gate that would make it safe) **and not of C-zwzepum0** (a false claim about another file's behaviour — that class has no second copy to derive from, whereas this one is a diff of two renders and is mechanically checkable, which is what makes it gateable where C-zwzepum0 was judged not to be). **EXPANDED 2026-08-17 by PR #100's fourth correction pass, with a measured second face of the same gap: the pool has no content guard AFTER promotion either.** That pass promoted `submit_and_push.md` and then mutated it — deleting the `RE-CHECK origin/main` bullet from the shared fragment outright — and **the entire suite stayed green (31 passed)**. Promotion converts a drift risk into a single-point-of-deletion risk: while text is duplicated, a one-sided edit is a divergence some detector can see; once shared, removing it takes it from every consumer at once and nothing diverges. This is not hypothetical maintenance worry — it is the same afternoon's measurement on the fragment that PR had just created to fix a missing-instruction defect. It applies to every fragment in the pool, not to that one — the count is deliberately not stated, because a bare figure here is the same race with the tree this file keeps losing. **THIS WIDENS C-lg7lpge9'S SCOPE and the widening is the point:** the row was opened about the ACT of promoting, and it now also covers the pool's content over its LIFETIME — a different trigger (any later edit, not a before/after snapshot) and a different mechanism (a standing content floor, not a one-time comparison). Ruling C-lg7lpge9 as a promotion-time check alone would leave the deletion half unsatisfied. **C-ll2j5ut7's note distinguishes itself from C-lg7lpge9 on the pre-expansion reading**, which was accurate when written; the two rows are still distinct, but that discriminator should be re-read against this sentence rather than taken at face value.
