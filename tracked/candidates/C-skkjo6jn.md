**The largest measurement this fleet has ever taken cannot be attributed to anything, and the instrument is why.** `_Sampler` polls every 2 s and keeps a running max, a running mean and nothing else — the series is discarded as it is read. **Measured 2026-08-11 by `python3 scripts/helpers/measure/replay_run_resources.py` over 17 fleet records** — a dated reading; [Phase 6 § Measurement](../../docs/development/memory-management-framework/phase6_read_what_it_writes.md) is the live one and this row does not track it:** one `build-draft` run peaked at **12.891 GiB** of anonymous memory against a **0.614 GiB** mean over 1761 samples, while **neither knob explains it** — it spawned **0** sub-agents and pulled **0.61 MiB** of tool results, both at or below the corpus median, and the next-highest peak in the corpus is **0.552 GiB**, a 23× gap. **Consequence:** figure 3 exists to separate sub-agent COUNT from content VOLUME, and the one run where the answer would matter most is a run where the answer is *neither*, with no way to ask *when*. A 21× peak-to-mean ratio says the spike was brief; nothing says which minute, which tool call, or whether it recurs. **The next occurrence produces exactly the same unattributable number**, which is the shape of the 2026-08-10 livelock one level down — an event nobody could locate. **PROPOSAL, not a defect:** the sampler does precisely what it says, every emitted field is correct, and no figure this phase built is wrong because of it. What does not exist is retention, which per [`finding-routing.md` § 4](../../docs/standards/finding-routing.md) makes it a candidate whatever its done-state looks like. **PRICED, because the phase brief asked for a price rather than a silent design-around.** The cheap arm has a done-state today and is genuinely small: keep a bounded ring of `(offset_seconds, anon, total, pids)` at the existing 2 s cadence and emit it only when `peak_anon` exceeds some multiple of `mean_total` — a 30-minute run is ~900 samples, and at four small integers that is well under 30 KB of JSONL for the rare record that earns it, with every other record unchanged. **The expensive arm is the one that makes it a candidate rather than a fix: WHERE the series lives.** A per-sample series is a different retention class from a per-run record — it is the first thing on this surface whose size scales with run LENGTH rather than with run COUNT, and the run log is machine-local, gitignored and pruned by nobody. **That is a Kind 3 retention question the surface has no rule for, and inventing one inside a build PR is exactly the "arrived at separately" failure C-73bf2gvm was raised to stop.** **Not an expansion of C-73bf2gvm:** that asks for the record types and their stores to be designed as a set; this is one concrete retention question that C-73bf2gvm's design would have to answer, and it is recorded so that design has a real instance to answer rather than a principle. **Not a defect against `resource_telemetry`:** its own docstring already states the limitation it has (`peak_anon` is sampling-dependent), and this is the adjacent limitation it does not state — recorded here, and now stated at the module, in the same change.
