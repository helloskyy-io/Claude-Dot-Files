## UNIVERSAL — verify a finding against the corpus authoritative for ITS OWN claim

**This is core, not type-specific. It binds every review type.**

> A finding is re-verified against the corpus that is **authoritative for its own claim**. A **standards** claim verifies against the standards corpus. A **code** claim verifies against code. A **planning** claim verifies against planning docs.

Measured failure: a reviewer recommended the operator rule on whether scheduled rotation should stage-before-invalidate, and stated it had *"re-verified against `origin/main` today"* — and it had, **against the code**. It never re-read the standard. That standard had been split the day before, operator-ratified and research-evidenced, into exactly the shape being recommended. The review asked for a decision made 24 hours earlier, survived two passes and nine days, and cost operator attention to unwind.

**Verifying against the wrong corpus reads exactly like verifying.** The confidence is identical; only the corpus is wrong. Before you record a finding, name which corpus decides it, then open that one.
