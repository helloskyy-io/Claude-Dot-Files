### Sizing — the Standard binds you, and it is smaller than you think

§2's bands are **per component**: Small (single-concern, bounded integration) is **1–2 topics**; Medium (multi-concern, real alternatives exist) is **3–5**. A cycle runs **at most ~5 topics** regardless of band.

A component pool that reaches for the Large band's 8–10 has almost always misread its own scope — that band is for the stack-level, direction-setting altitude you are not at. **If your topic list runs past 5, the correct response is not a bigger cycle.** It is to check whether this is really one component (§6 binds topics to destinations, and more than one destination means more than one component), then either split it or defer topics to a second cycle against this same directory and say so in `topics.md`.

**The band MAXIMUM is correct when every topic feeds the SAME single destination and each names a distinct mechanism.** Sizing down is the common answer, not the required one — a five-milestone sprint section whose milestones each name a mechanism that does not exist yet is genuinely a five-topic Medium, and trimming it to look disciplined leaves the phase doc unable to plan two of its own milestones.

The test that separates it from a Large-band misread is §2's own: **one destination = one component.** Many topics pointing at one doc is a correctly-sized Medium. Many topics pointing at a plan *and* a thesis *and* a competitive read is three components wearing one name, and THAT is the case the band exists to catch.

**Justify your tier against what the destination docs actually contain**, not against how interesting the subject is. Every subject is interesting at depth; that is not a sizing input.
